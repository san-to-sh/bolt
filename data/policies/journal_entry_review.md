# POL-004 — Journal Entry and Manual Adjustment Review Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-004
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15
**Owner:** Controller
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-06-01
**Applies To:** All manual journal entries and adjustments posted to the general ledger

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy requires independent review of all manual journal entries and adjustments to prevent and detect unauthorized, erroneous, or fraudulent entries in the general ledger.

## 2. Scope

All journal entries that are manually initiated, including:
- Standard recurring manual entries (accruals, deferrals, allocations)
- Non-standard or unusual entries
- Top-side adjustments
- Intercompany entries
- Correcting entries

Automated system-generated entries (e.g., subledger postings) are excluded from this policy but are subject to their own interface controls.

## 3. Preparer and Reviewer Requirements

- The **preparer** creates the journal entry with a documented business purpose, supporting calculation or source reference, and account coding.
- The **reviewer** must be a different individual from the preparer and must hold at least a Senior Accountant role or above.
- The reviewer verifies: (a) business purpose is valid, (b) amounts and accounts are correct, (c) supporting documentation is attached, and (d) the entry is posted to the correct period.

## 4. Timeliness

- Review must be completed and evidenced within **2 business days** of the entry's posting date.
- Entries posted without timely review are flagged in the monthly exception report.

## 5. Documentation

Each manual journal entry must include:
- Unique entry reference number
- Preparer identification and date
- Reviewer identification and sign-off date
- Business purpose narrative
- Supporting documentation or cross-reference

## 6. Monitoring and Testing

Internal Audit will test a sample of manual journal entries each year to verify that independent review was performed, properly evidenced, and completed within the required timeframe.
