# POL-001 — Purchase Order Approval Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-001
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15
**Owner:** VP Finance & Administration
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-06-01
**Applies To:** All departments initiating purchase orders

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the controls governing the approval of purchase orders (POs) to ensure that all expenditures are authorized by personnel with appropriate delegated authority before commitment to a vendor.

## 2. Scope

All purchase orders regardless of amount, payment method, or vendor category. Applies to both one-time purchases and recurring contractual commitments.

## 3. Approval Thresholds

| Tier | Amount Range | Required Approval |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Tier 1 | Below $10,000 | System auto-approval permitted; no manual sign-off required |
| Tier 2 | $10,000 – $24,999 | Manual approval by an authorized approver per the delegation-of-authority matrix (Section 4) |
| Tier 3 | $25,000 – $99,999 | Manual approval by Controller or above |
| Tier 4 | $100,000 and above | Manual approval by VP Finance & Administration or CFO |

Any PO at or above **$10,000** that carries only a system auto-approval stamp and lacks a manual sign-off by an authorized individual is a **control deviation**.

## 4. Delegation-of-Authority Matrix

An approver may only approve purchase orders within the dollar limit and categories assigned to them. Approval of a PO that exceeds the approver's delegated authority limit is a **control deviation**, even if the approver holds a senior role.

The current delegation-of-authority matrix is maintained by the Controller and reviewed annually. Any changes require written authorization from the VP Finance & Administration.

## 5. Documentation Requirements

Each approved PO must be retained with:
- The purchase requisition or request form identifying the requestor
- Evidence of the approver's sign-off (electronic signature, system log, or wet signature)
- Supporting documentation (quote, contract, or statement of work)

## 6. Monitoring and Testing

Internal Audit will test a sample of POs at or above $10,000 at least annually to verify:
- Manual approval is present
- The approver holds sufficient delegated authority for the amount
- Supporting documentation is complete

## 7. Exceptions

Any exception to this policy requires written pre-approval from the VP Finance & Administration and must be documented in the exception log maintained by the Controller.
