# POL-002 — Segregation of Duties Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-002
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-15
**Owner:** VP Finance & Administration
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-06-01
**Applies To:** All personnel involved in procurement, payment, or financial recording

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy requires that no single individual performs incompatible functions within the procure-to-pay cycle, thereby reducing the risk of error and fraud through independent checks.

## 2. Incompatible Function Pairs

The following function pairs must be performed by **different individuals**:

| Function A | Function B |
|-----------|-----------|
| Requesting a purchase | Approving the same purchase |
| Approving a purchase | Processing payment for the same purchase |
| Vendor master maintenance | Payment initiation to that vendor |
| Journal entry preparation | Journal entry review/posting for the same entry |

A transaction where the **requestor and the approver are the same person** is a segregation-of-duties violation and constitutes a **control deviation**.

## 3. System Enforcement

The ERP system shall enforce segregation by preventing the same user ID from completing both functions in a pair. Where system enforcement is not technically feasible, a compensating detective control (supervisory review within 5 business days) must be in place and documented.

## 4. Compensating Controls

When a temporary override is necessary (e.g., staff absence), the department head must:
- Document the reason for the override
- Ensure a compensating review is performed within 5 business days
- Notify Internal Audit of the override within 10 business days

## 5. Monitoring and Testing

Internal Audit will test the full population of purchase transactions at least annually to identify any instances where the same individual both requested and approved a transaction. Any violation is reported as a control deviation regardless of the dollar amount involved.
