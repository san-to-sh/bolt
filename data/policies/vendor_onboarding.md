# POL-003 — Vendor Onboarding Policy

**Policy ID:** POL-003
**Effective Date:** 2023-03-01
**Owner:** Procurement Manager
**Last Reviewed:** 2025-06-01
**Applies To:** All new vendor relationships and vendor reactivations

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy ensures that all vendors are properly vetted, approved, and documented before any payment is initiated, reducing the risk of fraudulent or fictitious vendor payments.

## 2. Required Onboarding Documentation

Before a vendor's first transaction may be processed, the following must be on file:

1. Completed vendor registration form (including legal name, address, tax ID)
2. Tax identification verification (W-9 or equivalent)
3. Banking information verified through an independent channel (not solely from the vendor's invoice)
4. Vendor approval form signed by the Procurement Manager and one additional approver
5. Screening results from the sanctions and debarment check

## 3. Approval Requirements

- New vendor approvals require sign-off by the **Procurement Manager** and one **department-level approver** from the requesting business unit.
- Reactivation of a previously inactive vendor follows the same approval path as a new vendor.

## 4. Vendor Master Maintenance

- Only authorized Procurement personnel may create or modify vendor master records.
- Changes to banking information require independent verification and a second approver.
- The vendor master is reviewed semi-annually; vendors with no activity in 18 months are flagged for deactivation.

## 5. Related-Entity Disclosure

Vendors must disclose any related entities, subsidiaries, or common ownership with other vendors in the master file. Undisclosed related-entity relationships identified during audit constitute a finding.

## 6. Monitoring and Testing

Internal Audit will test all newly onboarded vendors each year to verify that the required documentation was completed before the first payment was processed.
